# Rabobank Australia

> Rabobank Australia is the Australian arm of Rabobank Group, a Netherlands-headquartered cooperative bank and leading food & agribusiness lender. As an Australian ADI it is a mandated Consumer Data Right (CDR) data holder. Its only public, unauthenticated API surface is the CDR Product Reference Data (PRD) API, conforming to the DSB Consumer Data Standards. Authenticated CDR data sharing follows the accredited data recipient (ADR) model over OAuth2 / OpenID Connect (FAPI).

## APIs

- [Rabobank Australia CDR Product Reference Data API](https://www.rabobank.com.au/support/open-banking): Public, unauthenticated list of banking products at GET /banking/products (confirmed live, x-v 4, 34 products). Base: https://openbanking.api.rabobank.com.au/public/cds-au/v1
- [Rabobank Australia CDR Product Detail API](https://www.rabobank.com.au/support/open-banking): Full detail for a single product at GET /banking/products/{productId}.
